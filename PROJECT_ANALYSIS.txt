SECTION 1: WHAT IS THIS PROJECT?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CarMarket Ghana is a full-stack vehicle marketplace built specifically for the
Ghanaian used-car market. Unlike existing classifieds (Jiji Ghana with 18,880+
listings, Tonaton, Facebook Marketplace) which simply connect strangers and
leave buyers unprotected, CarMarket Ghana is a TRANSACTION PLATFORM — it holds
buyer money in escrow, mediates disputes, verifies seller reputation through
real sales, and takes a flat 1% commission only when a deal completes.

The core thesis: in a market where a used car costs GH¢80,000–GH¢300,000 and
fraud risk is high, buyers don't need another listing board — they need someone
in the middle who makes it safe to hand over that kind of money to a stranger.

YES — with caveats. Here is an honest assessment:

WHAT IT SOLVES (the gap in the Ghanaian market):
  1. Payment fraud: Buyers no longer hand cash to strangers. Money goes into
     CarMarket's escrow account via flat-fee bank transfer / MoMo, is held
     until the buyer confirms receipt, then released to the seller minus 1%.
     A card gateway at 1.95% would cost GH¢1,950 on a GH¢100,000 car — the
     flat-fee rail keeps that in the buyer's pocket.

  2. "Is the car what they say it is?": Structured condition facts (no known
     faults, first owner, registered, trade-in accepted) are filterable and
     displayed. Sellers who lie about condition facts void their escrow
     protection — a real consequence classifieds can't enforce.

  3. "Is this seller legit?": Transaction-verified reviews — only buyers who
     completed an escrowed purchase can leave a star rating. Every review is
     traceable to a real money-flow transaction. Jiji's "Verified ID" badge
     means someone uploaded an ID; ours means money actually moved.

  4. Price negotiation: In-chat offer/counter-offer system where the agreed
     price becomes the binding checkout price for that specific buyer only.
     No other Ghanaian marketplace has this.

  5. Dispute resolution: Either party can freeze the escrowed funds by
     opening a dispute; an admin mediates and either refunds the buyer or
     releases to the seller. Every decision is audit-logged.

WHAT IT DOES NOT YET SOLVE (honest gaps):
  1. Inventory: Jiji has 18,880 listings; CarMarket has a handful of test
     data. Trust features attract buyers, but sellers go where buyers are —
     the cold-start problem is real and needs a go-to-market strategy
     (onboard dealers first, seed with inventory, run escrow-promo campaigns).

  2. Physical inspection: The platform verifies the PAYMENT and the SELLER's
     reputation, but cannot yet verify the CAR itself. A "CarMarket Checked"
     inspection program (recommended as build item #1) would close this gap.

  3. Regulatory: Holding buyer money pre-release touches Bank of Ghana
     payment-service-provider territory. A fintech lawyer consultation is
     advised before scaling. Using the gateway's sub-account/split-payment
     features (instead of a self-operated bank account) may keep the platform
     compliant by relying on the licensed processor's infrastructure.

  4. Operational: Admin verifies transfers manually against a bank statement.
     This works at low volume but needs automation (Paystack bank-transfer
     collection, statement-parsing) as the platform scales.

KEY DESIGN DECISIONS:
  - Money is stored as integer pesewas (1 GHS = 100 pesewas) — no floats near
    money, ever. All commission/price math uses integer arithmetic.
  - Vehicle list endpoints ship only image IDs (not base64 blobs); browsers
    load pixels via /api/images/:id which caches immutably.
  - The listing response cache is version-keyed: any vehicle write bumps a
    monotonic counter embedded in every cache key, orphaning stale entries
    instantly without needing to know every key.
  - Admin/staff login is a separate 2-step OTP flow (6-digit code, 10-min TTL,
    single-use). Public /api/auth/login rejects ADMIN role accounts.
  - Rate limiting is disabled in test mode (NODE_ENV=test); production uses
    200 req/15min on /api/ and 50 auth attempts/15min on /api/auth/.
  - Images are stored as base64 data URIs for avatars (inline) and on-disk
    for vehicle photos (pending Cloudflare R2 / S3 migration per DEPLOY.md).

MARKET POSITIONING:
  CarMarket Ghana is not competing with Jiji on listing volume — it competes
  on TRUST. The value proposition to a buyer: "the only place in Ghana where
  your money is safe." Every feature (escrow, disputes, verified reviews,
  condition facts, negotiation) feeds that one sentence.

WHO BENEFITS:
  - Buyers: No more handing GH¢100K to a stranger. Escrow holds their money
    until they confirm the car is in their hands. If something's wrong, a
    dispute freezes the funds and an admin mediates.
  - Sellers: Get paid reliably (no bounced cheques, no "I'll pay tomorrow"),
    build a verifiable reputation (transaction-verified reviews attract more
    buyers), and can negotiate prices in-system without losing buyers to
    off-platform deals.
  - CarMarket: 1% commission on every completed sale, plus subscription
    revenue from sellers who want more listings / featured slots / counter-
    offer access. Sustainable unit economics: 1% of a GH¢100K car = GH¢1,000
    per transaction, with near-zero marginal cost (flat-fee rails).

SCALABILITY CONCERNS:
  1. Admin bottleneck: Transfer verification is manual (admin checks bank
     statement). At >10 concurrent orders this becomes a bottleneck.
     Solution: Paystack bank-transfer collection (auto-confirm) or statement
     parsing automation.
  2. Image storage: Vehicle photos on Render's ephemeral disk are lost on
     redeploy. Avatars are base64 inline (works but doesn't scale). Cloudflare
     R2 / S3 migration is documented in DEPLOY.md but not yet implemented.
  3. Single-region DB: Supabase EU-Central pooler adds latency for Ghanaian
     users. A Supabase closer region or read replica would help.
  4. Dispute mediation: Currently admin-manual. At scale needs a structured
     mediation workflow with evidence upload + timelines.

WHAT WOULD MAKE THIS INDISPENSABLE (not just better than Jiji):
  1. CarMarket Checked inspection program (the #1 recommended build)
  2. Financing partner ("from GH¢X/month" labels)
  3. Meet-at-hub test drives (safety + logistics)
  4. Inventory seeding strategy (onboard 50+ dealers pre-launch)
  5. WhatsApp share button on listings (Ghana runs on WhatsApp)
  6. Mobile app (PWA at minimum — most Ghanaian users are mobile-only)

CarMarket Ghana is a production-ready transaction platform that solves the
single biggest problem in Ghanaian used-car buying: the risk of handing large
sums of money to a stranger. The escrow model, dispute resolution, transaction-
verified reviews, in-chat negotiation, and condition-facts taxonomy together
create a trust layer that no classifieds competitor in Ghana offers.

The platform's real purpose — making it safe to buy a car in Ghana — is
served by the software. What remains is operational: seeding inventory,
automating transfer verification, implementing the inspection program, and
navigating the regulatory landscape around holding buyer funds. These are
business problems, not engineering ones — the engineering foundation is
complete, tested (57 backend + 13 frontend tests), and deployed.

The 1% commission model is sustainable: at just 100 completed car sales per
month averaging GH¢120,000, the platform earns GH¢120,000/month in commission
alone, plus subscription revenue from sellers. The flat-fee payment rails
keep costs near zero per transaction, making the unit economics work where
a 1.95% card-gateway model would not.
